Now we have to move Reebog through the same process but now, with a twist 

The finish flag, may appear anywhere.....randomly. So how do we do this?

We work with a given function at_goal() and the while loop.

So basically:

def turn_right():
    turn_left()
    turn_left()
    turn_left()
    
def jump():
    move()
    turn_left()
    move()
    turn_right()
    move()
    turn_right()
    move()
    turn_left()

while at_goal() != True:
    jump()

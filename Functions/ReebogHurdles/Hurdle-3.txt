In this hurdle, the distance between the walls is not symmetrical.
World info:
    The functions move() and turn_left().
    The conditions front_is_clear() or wall_in_front(), at_goal(), and their negation.
    How to use a while loop and an if statement.
    Your program should also be valid for worlds Hurdles 1 and Hurdles 2.

For this, we had to modify the jump() function and also introduce an if...else conditional to check for wall.
Alternatively, i beleive using a negation of front_is_clear where wall_in_front is also works.


-- the code--

def turn_right():
    turn_left()
    turn_left()
    turn_left()
    
def jump():
    turn_left()
    move()
    turn_right()
    move()
    turn_right()
    move()
    turn_left()

while not at_goal(): 
    if wall_in_front():
        jump()
    else:
        move()
